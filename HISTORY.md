## R41 · current showcase

Base is the default clean configuration. Equipped adds the removable roof and rear stowage boxes and the amber roof beacon. Both include Camo, Charcoal and neutral clay scenes.

R41 seats the driver’s visor handle against its mounts, restores the broad folded plate on the right rear deck, clears a triangular overlap on the left front landing, and gives the side guards and steps a textured nonslip surface with smooth painted edges.

[R41 gallery](galleries/R41.md) · [Base model](models/R41-Base.md) · [Equipped model](models/R41-Equipped.md) · [Rendering and verification notes](images/r41/RENDERING.md)

R41 also has fresh six-second Base and Equipped turntables in Camo and Charcoal, alongside all 15 supplied still renders. [View the current showcase](README.md).
