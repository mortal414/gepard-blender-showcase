## R47 · current showcase

Base is the default clean model; Equipped adds optional roof boxes, rear stowage and the beacon. The gallery uses eight supplied model-only PNGs per version, including exterior and interior views. Both reviewed source files retain Camo, Charcoal and neutral clay scenes with all 248 images packed.

R47 fixes the driver’s cabin lining, four crew-ceiling fasteners, two roof-edge intersections and a fold in the right front fender.

[R47 gallery](galleries/R47.md) · [Base model](models/R47-Base.md) · [Equipped model](models/R47-Equipped.md) · [Rendering notes](images/r47/RENDERING.md)

## R42

Base remains the default clean configuration; Equipped adds roof boxes, rear stowage and the beacon. The verified delivery contains both finishes and neutral clay, with 247 packed images in each native file.

R42 refines the gun-arm shoulders, locker and rear lamp supports, visor/pedestal details and exterior surface treatment. The gallery contains 24 Base and 9 Equipped model-only renders.

[R42 gallery](galleries/R42.md) · [Base model](models/R42-Base.md) · [Equipped model](models/R42-Equipped.md) · [Rendering notes](images/r42/RENDERING.md)

## R41

Base is the default clean configuration. Equipped adds the removable roof and rear stowage boxes and the amber roof beacon. Both include Camo, Charcoal and neutral clay scenes.

R41 seats the driver’s visor handle against its mounts, restores the broad folded plate on the right rear deck, clears a triangular overlap on the left front landing, and gives the side guards and steps a textured nonslip surface with smooth painted edges.

[R41 gallery](galleries/R41.md) · [Base model](models/R41-Base.md) · [Equipped model](models/R41-Equipped.md) · [Rendering and verification notes](images/r41/RENDERING.md)

R41 also has fresh six-second Base and Equipped turntables in Camo and Charcoal, alongside all 15 supplied still renders. [R41 overview archive](R41-ARCHIVE.md).
