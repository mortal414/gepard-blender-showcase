# Gepard 1A2

A detailed Blender model of the Gepard 1A2, including the exterior, driver’s cabin and crew compartment. **R47 is the current reviewed model checkpoint.**

The focus is the model’s form, construction and interior. **Paint and skins are provisional and will be revised.** Both Blender files include a neutral clay scene for inspecting the geometry.
